Weights above are unsigned. In signed interpretation the leftmost weight is negative.

  1. Width: 4; retain only the lowest 4 bits.
  2. A: 0111 = unsigned 7, signed 7
  3. B: 0001 = unsigned 1, signed 1
  4. Unsigned sum: 7 + 1 = 8; maximum 15.
  5. Wrapped result: (8) modulo 16 = 8.
  6. Result bits: 1000.
  7. Signed sum: 7 + 1 = 8.
  8. Signed range: -8 through 7.
  9. Unsigned overflow: False; signed overflow: True.
  10. Leading bit is 1: signed result = 8 - 16 = -8.
